# AtliQ Hardwares – Providing Insights to Management in Consumer Goods Domain
This project was completed as part of the Resume Project Challenge (Challenge #4 – Consumer Goods Domain) by Codebasics. 

###  Problem Statement  
AtliQ Hardware is a global manufacturer of computer hardware products, including personal computers, laptops, and accessories, with operations spanning multiple international markets.
As part of its data-driven expansion strategy, AtliQ initiated a SQL Analytics Challenge to evaluate candidates’ ability to derive actionable business insights from real-world datasets.
The objective was to analyze sales performance, product portfolio growth, and customer/channel efficiency across three major product divisions and sales channels:

Product Divisions:
- Peripherals & Accessories
- Network & Storage
- Personal Computers

Distribution Channels:
- Retailer
- Direct
- Distributors

### Key Learnings
  1. Advanced SQL querying and data transformation
  2. Usage of Subqueries, CTEs (Common Table Expressions), and Window Functions
  3. Creation of User-Defined Functions, Views, and Stored Procedures
  4. Effective data storytelling and presentation skills for management reporting

### Ad_Hoc Requests
1. Provide the list of markets in which customer "Atliq Exclusive" operates its business in the APAC region.
2. What is the percentage of unique product increase in 2021 vs. 2020? 
3. Provide a report with all the unique product counts for each segment and sort them in descending order of product counts.
4. Follow-up: Which segment had the most increase in unique products in 2021 vs 2020?
5. Get the products that have the highest and lowest manufacturing costs. 
6. Generate a report which contains the top 5 customers who received an average high pre_invoice_discount_pct for the fiscal year 2021 and in the Indian market.
7. Get the complete report of the Gross sales amount for the customer “Atliq Exclusive” for each month . This analysis helps to get an idea of low and high-performing months and take strategic decisions.
8. In which quarter of 2020, got the maximum total_sold_quantity?
9. Which channel helped to bring more gross sales in the fiscal year 2021 and the percentage of contribution?
10. Get the Top 3 products in each division that have a high total_sold_quantity in the fiscal_year 2021?

### Insights 
- AtliQ Exclusive operates in 8 key markets within the APAC region, indicating a strong regional footprint.
- Unique product offerings grew by 36.3% (2021 vs 2020) — signaling expansion in product variety and innovation.
- Continuous innovation in underperforming segments could enhance overall portfolio balance.
- AQ HOME Allin 1 Gen 2 (Personal Computer) has the highest manufacturing cost, while AQ Masterwired x1 Ms (Mouse) is the most cost-efficient product.
- Flipkart, Viveks, and Ezone emerged as top customers, each contributing roughly 30%+ of total sales, highlighting their strategic importance for partnerships and promotions.
- March 2020 recorded the lowest sales (₹1.42M) due to the pandemic, whereas November consistently saw sales peaks (₹28.5M) aligned with festive seasons. This trend indicates an opportunity to strengthen marketing and supply readiness ahead of festive periods.
- Q1 of FY2020 saw the highest sales volume, aligning with seasonal demand patterns as AtliQ’s fiscal year begins in September.
- The Retailer channel accounted for ~73% of total sales, followed by Direct stores (16%), emphasizing the importance of maintaining retailer relationships.
- The Network & Storage division leads in total units sold, suggesting robust market demand and efficient distribution. Inventory and supply chain optimization in this division could further prevent overstock or stock-out scenarios.
