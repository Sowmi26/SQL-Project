# AtliQ Hardwares – Providing Insights to Management in Consumer Goods Domain

###  Project Overview  
AtliQ Hardwares is one of the leading computer hardware producers in India, with a growing presence in other countries. However, the management team noticed a lack of data-driven insights to support quick and informed decision-making.  

To overcome this challenge, the analytics team leveraged **SQL** for data exploration and analysis, followed by **Power BI** for interactive visualization and storytelling. The goal was to transform raw business data into actionable insights that empower strategic decisions across sales, marketing, and operations.  

---

###  Problem Statement  
Despite its rapid growth, AtliQ Hardwares lacked clear visibility into key performance areas such as product sales, customer performance, discounts, and regional demand patterns.  
Management needed analytical insights to:  
- Understand sales performance across different regions and time periods.  
- Identify top-performing customers, channels, and products.  
- Monitor cost structures and product diversity over time.  
- Detect trends to improve sales and marketing strategies.  

---

###  Key Objectives  
1. Identify key markets and customer operations across the APAC region.  
2. Measure product growth trends between 2020 and 2021.  
3. Analyze sales distribution by segment and channel.  
4. Determine cost patterns across different products.  
5. Highlight high- and low-performing products, customers, and regions.  
6. Support management in making data-backed strategic decisions.  

---

###  Tools & Technologies  
- **SQL** → Data extraction, cleaning, and analysis  
- **Power BI** → Interactive dashboards and storytelling  
- **Excel** → Validation and data summaries (optional)  

---

### Data Analysis Workflow  
1. **Data Understanding** – Study the datasets and relationships (sales, customers, products, markets).  
2. **Data Cleaning & Preparation** – Handle missing values, date formatting, and data type corrections.  
3. **Exploratory Analysis using SQL** –  
   - Identify key metrics like gross sales, discount %, and sold quantities.  
   - Compare performance year-over-year and by channel.  
4. **Visualization in Power BI** –  
   - Build visuals to represent insights clearly (sales trends, product performance, channel contribution).  
5. **Insights & Recommendations** – Share findings with stakeholders for data-informed decisions.  

---

### Key Insights  
- **Regional Analysis:** “AtliQ Exclusive” operates in multiple APAC markets, showing diverse sales coverage.  
- **Product Diversity:** Unique product count increased notably in 2021 vs. 2020, indicating product line expansion.  
- **Segment Growth:** Certain segments showed a strong increase in unique products, suggesting focused R&D efforts.  
- **Cost Analysis:** Wide variation between the highest and lowest manufacturing costs highlights optimization opportunities.  
- **Customer Insights:** Top 5 customers in India received significant pre-invoice discounts, influencing gross margins.  
- **Sales Trend:** Monthly sales analysis revealed seasonal peaks and low-performing periods for “AtliQ Exclusive.”  
- **Channel Performance:** A specific sales channel contributed the highest share of gross sales in FY 2021.  
- **Top Products:** Each division had its top 3 best-sellers based on sold quantity in 2021.  

---

### Recommendations  
- Focus marketing and stock planning around high-performing quarters.  
- Reevaluate discount policies for top customers to maintain profitability.  
- Optimize manufacturing costs by benchmarking high-cost products.  
- Strengthen channels that drive maximum sales revenue.  
- Continue expanding in APAC regions with proven customer success.  
- Develop data dashboards for real-time business monitoring.
